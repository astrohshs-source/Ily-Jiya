a letter for you

everything, and only you

Dearest Baccha, yesterday may have been Girlfriend's Day, but I don't think there should be just one day to celebrate you.

For me, every morning begins because you exist. Every night ends with thoughts of you. Every ordinary day becomes extraordinary simply because you're in it.

So today, on 2 August, I wanted to tell you something I don't ever want you to forget. No matter what happened before me, no matter what happened yesterday, no matter what happens tomorrow — you matter.

Not your mistakes. Not your fears. Not your past. Not the things that keep you awake at night. You.

The girl I fell in love with on 22 November 2025.

I never want you to think I'll keep asking "why did you do that," "why didn't you tell me," "why did things happen this way." Because if we're choosing each other, then the past isn't a weapon. It's just a chapter that brought you to me.

I don't want to spend my life questioning your scars. I want to spend my life helping them heal. If something hurts you, let it hurt me too. If something makes you smile, let me be the first one smiling because of your happiness.

If someday life becomes difficult, don't stand alone. Stand beside me. We'll suffer together, fight together, heal together. That's what love means to me.

People say love is about letting go. Maybe they're right sometimes. But if loving you means walking away while you're hurting, then I don't want that kind of love. I want the kind that stays — the kind that holds your hand when everything is falling apart, the kind that whispers "I'm still here," even on the hardest days. Especially on the hardest days.

You've become my peace. Jaan. Sukoon. My safest place.

Sometimes I try to imagine life without you, and I can't — because somewhere along the way, you stopped feeling like a part of my life. You became a part of my soul. You're in my thoughts when I wake up, in my prayers before I sleep, in every future I imagine.

Whether life takes us to Germany, somewhere else in Europe, or even Dubai — I don't care about the destination. Home isn't a country anymore. Home is wherever you're standing.

I dream of the day I can proudly introduce you as my wife — not because marriage is a title, but because I want the privilege of spending every ordinary moment with you. Morning coffees. Late-night talks. Random grocery shopping. Holding your hand while crossing the street. Growing old beside you. These tiny moments are my biggest dreams.

I promise you something today. I'll never intentionally make you cry. I'll never let you feel alone if I can help it. I'll never stop trying to understand you. I'll choose conversations over silence, understanding over assumptions, us over my ego. Every single time.

You don't have to become perfect for me. You don't have to hide your fears. You don't have to pretend to be strong. Just be you — because that's the girl I fell in love with, and that's the woman I'll keep choosing every single day.

If someday people ask me what the best decision of my life was, I'll smile. Because it'll always be choosing you.

Every sunrise belongs to you. Every heartbeat whispers your name. Every tomorrow, I hope it has you beside me.

Thank you for existing. Thank you for loving me. Thank you for making my world brighter without even realizing it. You'll never have to earn my love. You'll never have to prove your worth. You already are my greatest blessing.

I love you more than words can ever explain.

— forever yours
